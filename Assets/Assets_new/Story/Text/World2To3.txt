As it always does, peace came too late.
The Earth was boiling over.
All the good rocks had been used up making space ships to destroy other space ships.
Yes all of them.
Somehow.

And there wasn't enough left to spread to other stars.
Peak space-gas had long past.
All of humanity switched over to working on the problem of their species' future---the ones who weren't too busy, and barely anyone was.
But the other stars just seemed too far away.

Studying rocks from the far reaches of space, a brilliant team of scientists discovered a new element, picolinium.
It was green and it glowed, and it smelled like hope.
And it also smelled like pickles.

All the atoms in picolinium were backwards and it could bend space.
People tried to use it to make time machines and stop the wars before everyone used up all the good rocks, but obviously it didn't work.
People really liked to fight back then.

Centuries passed, and when all hope seemed lost, they figured out how to use picolinium to make a ship go really fast.
With the Prometheus Drive, a ship could jump to another star much faster than a regular gas powered one.
The only problem was there was only enough left for a single ship.
They called it the Stellar Flame.
